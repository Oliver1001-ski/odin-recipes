CARBONARA RECIPE

Crispy fried chicken is a culinary masterpiece defined by its contrast of textures and rich flavors. The exterior features a golden-brown crust, shattered easily with a bite to reveal deep ridges of crunchy, seasoned batter. Infused with a blend of spices like garlic, paprika, and black pepper, this outer shell locks in the natural juices of the meat beneath.

Inside, the meat is incredibly tender and succulent, pulling away effortlessly from the bone. Each bite delivers a savory rush of warmth, where the rich rendering of chicken fat balances perfectly with the seasoned crunch of the skin. It is a comforting, sensory experience that leaves a lingering taste of savory goodness and a craving for the next bite.

  • Chicken
  • Buttermilk
  • Flour
  • Cornstarch
  • Seasonings
  • Oil
  1. Marinate the chicken: Soak chicken pieces in seasoned buttermilk for at least 1 hour or overnight in the fridge.
  2. Mix the dredge: Whisk together the flour, cornstarch, and seasonings in a large shallow bowl.
  3. Coat the pieces: Dredge each piece of chicken in the flour mixture, dip it back into the buttermilk, and coat it in the flour again for a double crunch.
  4. Heat the oil: Fill a heavy pot or deep fryer with oil and heat it to 350°F (175°C).
  5. Fry until golden: Fry the chicken in batches for 12 to 15 minutes (until the internal temperature reaches 165°F / 74°C), then drain on a wire rack.
